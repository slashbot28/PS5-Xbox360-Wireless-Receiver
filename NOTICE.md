## PoorDS4

The PS5 `libScePad` game-side import bridge (`payload/wireless_ds4.c`,
`payload/wireless_ds4.h`, and supporting header files in `payload/`) is derived
from **PoorDS4** by ItsBlurf (https://github.com/ItsBlurf/PoorDS4), licensed
under GPL-3.0-or-later, with modifications (`X360W_EXTERNAL_SOURCE`) to allow
external USB Xbox 360 Wireless Receiver slots to drive logged-in PS5 users'
`ScePad` handles and relay game vibration feedback over USB.

## ps5-payload-dev/sdk

Built against the actively maintained **ps5-payload-dev/sdk**
(https://github.com/ps5-payload-dev/sdk).
